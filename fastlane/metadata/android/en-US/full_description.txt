AutoPlay automatically resumes your paused or stopped music when you plug in headphones.

How it works:
- Runs as a persistent foreground service
- Detects wired, Bluetooth (A2DP/LE), USB, and hearing-aid headphones
- Finds the most recently active media session and sends Play
- Falls back to media-button broadcast if needed
- Restarts on boot

Features:
- Single-tap ON/OFF toggle
- Adjustable resume delay (0-3 seconds)
- Headphone-type filtering
- Per-app exclusion list
- Smart Resume learning - adapts delay to your patterns
- Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile
- Light / dark / system theme
- Backup / restore settings via clipboard
- No ads, no tracking, no network permission
- Fully offline

Open source: https://github.com/fra/autoplay (MIT)
Requires Android 8.0+, no special hardware.
